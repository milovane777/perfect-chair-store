Perfect Chair is a notebook for hairdressers that never leaves your device.

No account. No server. No internet permission at all. Every client record, every formula and every photo stays in your phone's private storage — which is also why it opens instantly and works in a basement salon with no signal.

WHAT YOU CAN KEEP
• Client profiles with hair history, scalp sensitivity, allergy and patch-test notes
• Service records: formula, developer volume, processing time, price, memo
• Before and after photos, side by side
• Freehand marking on a head diagram for sectioning and placement
• Step-by-step process diagrams with timers that ring even when the app is closed
• Tags, search, and a calendar of everything you have done

BUILT FOR THE CHAIR
• Patch-test reminders 48 hours ahead, so you never have to guess
• Developer volume and percentage converted both ways
• Repeat a past service as a new one in two taps
• Portrait on the phone, full screen on an iPad

YOUR DATA IS YOURS
• Make a backup file whenever you like and keep it where you choose
• Restore it on a new phone in one step
• Delete the app and everything goes with it — there is no copy anywhere else

Available in ten languages: English, Chinese, Hindi, Spanish, Arabic, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Korean and French.

No ads. No subscription. No tracking.
